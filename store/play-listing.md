## Full description (max 4000)
Meet Brainy Owl! 🦉 Kids Brain Games is a colorful collection of 12 brain games for kids that make memory, logic, math and spelling feel like play.

Every game has 3 difficulty levels – Easy, Medium and Hard – with 30 levels each. That's over 1,000 levels, so children keep finding a fresh challenge as they grow.

🧠 12 BRAIN GAMES IN ONE KIDS APP
• Memory Match – flip the cards and find all the pairs
• Simon Says – copy the colors and sounds to train focus
• Connect the Dots – plan ahead and draw the picture
• Shape Puzzle – fit the shapes together
• Odd One Out – spot what doesn't belong
• Patterns – finish the sequence
• Math Pop – pop the bubbles with the right answer
• Maze Runner – find the way out
• Spell It – build words letter by letter
• Spot the Difference – sharpen those eyes
• Sudoku for Kids – first logic puzzles made simple
• Count & Tap – learn to count with fun taps

⭐ EARN STARS, COLLECT STICKERS
Win stars on every level and unlock a Sticker Book of 60 cute animals, dinosaurs and sea creatures. A daily streak keeps kids coming back to learn a little every day.

👶 MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
• Big, bright buttons and simple instructions
• Friendly owl guide with cheerful praise
• Gentle original music and sound effects (easy to turn off)
• Works offline – great for car rides, flights and waiting rooms
• No sign-in, no account and no personal data collected

👨‍👩‍👧 PARENT FRIENDLY
• Child-safe ads only: G-rated and non-personalized
• Settings and external links are behind a grown-up check
• Progress is saved on the device and can be reset at any time

WHY KIDS AND PARENTS LOVE IT
• Builds memory, focus, logic, planning and problem solving
• Practises early math, counting, shapes, patterns and spelling
• Short levels fit into busy days, and difficulty grows with your child
